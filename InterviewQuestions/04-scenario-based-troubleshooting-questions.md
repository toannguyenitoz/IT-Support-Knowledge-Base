# Scenario-Based Troubleshooting Questions

## 1. A user says Outlook is disconnected. What do you do?

**Sample answer:**

Check internet/VPN, Outlook work offline mode, Outlook on the web, account password/MFA, Outlook connection status, Safe Mode, profile and Microsoft 365 service health.

---

## 2. A user cannot access a shared folder. What do you check?

**Sample answer:**

Confirm path, network access, VPN, DNS, share permissions, NTFS permissions, group membership and whether other users can access it.

---

## 3. A user cannot print. What do you check?

**Sample answer:**

Check printer power/network, default printer, print queue, print spooler service, driver, printer mapping and whether others can print.

---

## 4. A user reports missing emails. What do you check?

**Sample answer:**

Search all mailboxes, Junk, Deleted Items, Archive, rules, Focused Inbox, Outlook web, message trace and quarantine.

---

## 5. Teams meeting button is missing in Outlook. What do you check?

**Sample answer:**

Check Teams desktop app, Outlook COM add-ins, Microsoft Teams Meeting Add-in, restart Teams then Outlook, and repair Microsoft 365 Apps if needed.
