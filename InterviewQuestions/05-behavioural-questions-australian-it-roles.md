# Behavioural Questions for Australian IT Roles

## 1. Tell me about yourself.

**Sample answer:**

I am an Adelaide-based IT Support and Systems Administration candidate with 20+ years of enterprise technology experience and current hands-on training in Microsoft 365, Windows, networking and systems administration at TAFE SA.

---

## 2. Why are you interested in IT Support in Australia?

**Sample answer:**

I enjoy helping users, solving practical technical problems and documenting solutions. I am focused on applying my enterprise IT background in the Australian IT workplace.

---

## 3. How do you handle pressure?

**Sample answer:**

I stay calm, prioritise by impact, communicate clearly, document actions and escalate early when required.

---

## 4. Describe a time you worked with non-technical users.

**Sample answer:**

In enterprise banking IT, I regularly supported business users by translating technical issues into clear explanations and practical solutions.

---

## 5. What are your strengths?

**Sample answer:**

Structured troubleshooting, documentation, calm communication, enterprise IT experience, willingness to learn and practical support mindset.
