# IT Support and Service Desk Questions

## 1. How do you handle a user who cannot log in?

**Sample answer:**

I would confirm the exact error, check whether the account is locked or disabled, verify password status, test another device or browser if relevant, and document the steps taken. I would also communicate clearly with the user and escalate if identity policies or admin permissions are required.

---

## 2. What information do you collect when creating a ticket?

**Sample answer:**

User details, device name, application/service affected, error message, time issue started, business impact, troubleshooting already attempted, screenshots, and whether other users are affected.

---

## 3. How do you prioritise support tickets?

**Sample answer:**

I prioritise based on business impact, urgency, number of affected users, security risk and agreed SLA.

---

## 4. How do you explain technical issues to non-technical users?

**Sample answer:**

I avoid jargon, explain the impact in simple terms, confirm understanding, and provide clear next steps.

---

## 5. What makes good IT documentation?

**Sample answer:**

Clear symptoms, root cause, resolution steps, commands used, screenshots where appropriate, escalation notes and user communication.
